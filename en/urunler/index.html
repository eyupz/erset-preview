Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between platform scaffolding (ERP) and hoists (ERY)?
ERP platform scaffolding is a mast-climbing work platform that carries workers and materials along a façade for exterior works. ERY hoists are vertical transport systems that move material and personnel between floors on a construction site.
What working heights and capacities do Erset products reach?
The 2022 catalogue lists ERP platforms with capacities from 1,500 to 3,000 kg and heights up to 250 m. ERY capacities vary by model, from 250 kg for the tower crane lift to 5,000 kg for the material transport series.
Are Erset products CE certified?
The Erset catalogue includes CE conformity documents. For the current certificate covering a specific model, please contact Erset directly.
How can I get a detailed datasheet or a price quote?
Contact the Erset engineering team by phone, WhatsApp or email at info@erset.com.tr. We prepare the datasheet and configuration that best fits your project.
